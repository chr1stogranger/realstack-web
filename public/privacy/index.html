Privacy policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

  • We use your data only to run RealStack for you. We don't sell it, rent it or use it for advertising.
  • Your loan files and your clients' information belong to your workspace. Other workspaces can't see them.
  • We read your email and calendar only when you connect them, and only to show and send mail inside RealStack.
  • Card payments go through Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

Who we are and what this covers

RealStack is operated by Ocean Beach Ventures LLC, San Francisco, California ("RealStack", "we", "us"). This policy covers RealStack Ops (ops.realstack.app), the loan officer side of Blueprint (blueprint.realstack.app) and this website.

It applies to loan officers, assistants, processors and anyone else with a RealStack login. Home buyers and borrowers who use Blueprint are covered by the Blueprint privacy policy.

Information we collect

  • Your account: your name, email address and profile picture from the Google or Microsoft account you sign in with, or just your email address if you sign in with an emailed link. We also keep your role and which workspaces you belong to.
  • Your book of business: the loans, leads, contacts, partners, notes, tasks and documents you add to RealStack or sync from your loan origination system (such as Arive). This often includes your clients' personal and financial information.
  • Email and calendar, if you connect them: with your permission we read messages and calendar events from your Gmail or Outlook account so RealStack can show the ones related to your loans, and we send email from your account when you click Send. See "Google and Microsoft data" below.
  • Documents you upload: files such as mortgage statements and lender guidelines, kept in private storage.
  • Billing: your subscription status, plan and invoice history from Stripe. Stripe collects your card or bank details directly.
  • Usage and security records: sign-in times and an audit log of changes in your workspace, kept to protect your account and help with support.
  • The waitlist and free-trial forms on this site: the name, email address, phone number and plan you enter.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep each workspace separate.
  • To show your pipeline, sync it with the systems you connect, and send the emails and updates you ask RealStack to send.
  • To read documents you upload and fill in fields for you (see "AI features" below).
  • To bill your subscription, and to email you about your account, your trial, payments and changes to RealStack.
  • To keep RealStack secure, fix problems and answer your questions.

We don't sell or rent personal information, we don't use it for advertising, and we don't share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Your workspace and your clients' information

Each loan officer's book is a separate workspace. Only people the workspace owner adds can see it. RealStack staff look at a workspace only to support it, keep it secure, or when the law requires.

When you put your clients' information into RealStack, you decide what goes in and why. We handle it on your behalf to provide the service. You're responsible for having the right to share it with us and for meeting the privacy rules that apply to your business.

Google and Microsoft data

If you connect Gmail or Google Calendar, or an Outlook mailbox or calendar, RealStack uses that access only to show your mail and events inside RealStack and to send the messages you choose to send. Mail and events are fetched when you open them. We don't keep a copy of your mailbox, and we don't store the access token from Google or Microsoft on our servers. Your browser holds it and sends it with each request.

RealStack's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We don't use Google or Microsoft user data for advertising, we don't sell it, we don't let people read it except as described here, and we don't use it to train AI models.

You can disconnect at any time inside RealStack, or remove RealStack's access from your Google or Microsoft account settings.

AI features

Some features read documents for you, such as filling in a current loan from a mortgage statement or summarizing a lender's guidelines. Those documents are sent to Anthropic, which provides the Claude AI model, only when you use the feature. Anthropic processes them to return the result under terms that don't allow it to train its models on that content.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the companies that help us run RealStack, and only what they need:

  • Vercel: hosts the apps and this website.
  • Supabase: database and private file storage.
  • Stripe: subscriptions and payments.
  • Resend: sends sign-in links and automated emails.
  • Google and Microsoft: sign-in, plus email and calendar when you connect them.
  • Anthropic: reads the documents you ask it to (see above).
  • Services you connect, such as Arive or Homebot, to sync the data you ask us to sync.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect RealStack, our users or others, or as part of a sale or merger of the business, in which case this policy keeps applying to it.

Email link tracking

Links in automated emails, and in email signatures where the sender turns it on, may go through a short redirect on ops.realstack.app so we can count that the link was followed. We record the link, the time, the browser type and, for links sent to a known person, that person's email address. We don't store IP addresses, only a one-way scrambled version that can't identify you or where you are. We don't track whether emails are opened. Unsubscribe links never go through the redirect.

Cookies and browser storage

RealStack keeps your sign-in and some cached data in your browser so pages load quickly. We don't use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. This website doesn't use analytics.

Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Loan documents you upload, such as statements and lender guidelines, are kept in private storage that's only reachable through short-lived signed links. Images you choose to publish, such as an email signature photo, are public so recipients can see them. Server keys never reach the browser, and every request is checked against the workspace you belong to. No system is perfectly secure, so if we learn of a breach that affects your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

How long we keep it

We keep your workspace data while your account is open. If you cancel, your data stays so you can come back, unless you ask us to delete it. Email us and we'll delete your workspace and its documents within 30 days, except records we must keep by law, such as billing records. Copies in backups are removed as the backups expire.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal information. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days. If you live in California or another place with privacy laws that give you these rights, we honor them. We don't sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising.

If a borrower asks you about their information in RealStack, we'll help you answer.

Children

RealStack is for mortgage professionals and isn't meant for anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we make a significant change, we'll email account owners before it takes effect and update the date above.
