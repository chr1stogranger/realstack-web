Billing and cancellation policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

  • 14 days free to try RealStack Ops. No card needed to start.
  • Billed monthly, in advance, until you cancel.
  • Cancel anytime and keep access until the end of the month you paid for.
  • No refunds for partial months or unused time.

What you pay

RealStack is priced per loan officer workspace. A workspace is one loan officer's book, along with the assistants and processors who work in it. Current prices are on our pricing page.

Founding members pay $99 per month for Ops + Blueprint. The founding-member rate is open to the first 25 workspaces through March 31, 2027, whichever comes first. That rate stays the same for as long as your subscription stays active. If you cancel and subscribe again later, the price at that time applies.

Prices are in US dollars. Sales tax is added only where the law requires it.

Free trial

A new workspace can use RealStack Ops free for 14 days, with no card required. Ops shows how many trial days are left. To keep using it after the trial, the workspace owner subscribes from My Account → Billing.

Your first payment is charged when you subscribe, even if trial days remain. If the trial ends without a subscription, the workspace pauses until someone subscribes. Nothing in it is deleted.

How billing works

Your subscription renews automatically every month, on the same day of the month that you first subscribed. We charge the card or account you saved at checkout. Payments are processed by Stripe, so RealStack never sees or stores your full card number.

You can update your card, see your invoices and download receipts any time from My Account → Billing → Manage billing.

Cancelling

Cancel any time from My Account → Billing → Manage billing, or email us and we'll do it for you. There are no cancellation fees and no contracts.

When you cancel, you keep full access until the end of the billing period you've already paid for, and you won't be charged again. After that, the workspace pauses. Your data isn't deleted when you cancel, and you can subscribe again to pick up where you left off.

Refunds

Payments are non-refundable. We don't refund or credit partial months, unused time, or months in which you didn't use RealStack.

If we charge you by mistake, for example twice for the same month, we'll refund the extra charge in full. Just email us. Where the law gives you a right to a refund, that right still applies.

Failed payments

If a payment doesn't go through, your workspace stays open while Stripe tries the card again over the following days, and Ops shows a reminder to update it. If payment still fails, the workspace pauses until the card is updated. Nothing in it is deleted, and your clients' Blueprint links keep working.

Changes to this policy

If we change prices or this policy, we'll tell current subscribers by email at least 30 days before the change affects them.
